Volume Boost

Adds a boost slider next to the volume control in the web player and the Jellyfin Desktop app. Keyboard: Shift + ↑ / ↓. Double-click the slider to reset to 100%.

100 = no boost, 300 = up to 3× as loud. Values above ~400% will mostly just push into the limiter.
Slider and keyboard step size.

After saving, reload the Jellyfin web page in your browser for changes to apply.
